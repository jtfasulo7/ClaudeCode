Who it's for

And, just as usefully, who it is not for.

  • People who keep running into peptide claims online and want to judge them for themselves.
  • People who would rather understand the mechanism than memorise a conclusion.
  • People happy to be told that the evidence is thin, when it is thin.
  • Not for anyone looking for dosing instructions, protocols, or somewhere to buy. You will not find those here.
  • Not a substitute for a doctor. Nothing here is medical advice, and nobody here is your clinician.
