4Our social media publishing tool

We publish short-form video to our own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. We use an internal tool to do it, so that one finished video can be posted to several of our own accounts without repeating the work by hand. This section explains exactly what that tool touches.

The tool handles our own accounts and our own content — not yours. It does not collect, store or process personal data about anyone who views, follows, likes or comments on our posts, and it is not offered to community members as a service.

What it stores

  • Access and refresh tokens for our own social accounts. These are issued by each platform when the account holder authorises the tool through that platform's own login flow. They are stored encrypted and are used only to publish and to read the account's own posting settings.
  • The video and captions we are publishing, held only as long as needed to hand them to the platforms.

What it reads from the platforms

Immediately before we publish, the tool asks the platform for the connected account's own settings: the account name, which audiences that account is allowed to post to, the maximum video length, and whether comments, Duet or Stitch are switched off on that account. This is used to show the correct options to the person publishing. It is not stored, and it is not used for analytics.

Where content goes

  • To the social platforms — the video, its caption, the audience chosen and the commercial-content declaration, sent through each platform's official publishing API.
  • To Anthropic — to help draft a caption for each platform, still frames taken from the video and any notes we type are sent to the Claude API. A person reviews and edits every caption before it is published. We do not send member data.

Control

Nothing is published without a person choosing the audience and pressing publish — there is no unattended or scheduled posting. Authorisation for any connected account can be withdrawn at any time from that platform's own settings, which immediately stops the tool being able to post to it.

Access to our internal tools is password protected. Credentials for our own social accounts are encrypted at rest and are never exposed to a browser. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously and will tell you promptly if something happens that affects you.

9Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to receive it in a portable form. Email us and we will action it — we will not make you justify the request.

Much of your community data sits in your Skool account, which you can manage or delete directly with Skool.

If you are in the UK you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office; in the EEA, to your local supervisory authority. We would rather you came to us first.
